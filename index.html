    Vehicle
    
      
    
    
    

    
    Controls: W/S throttle and brake, A/D steer, Space boost, Shift drift, R reset to the road, C camera, 0 reset camera view, V rear-view mirror. You can also double-click the game view to centre the camera. Gamepad and touch controls work too.
    
    
    Derby: ram the rival cars. Every crash damages the part of the car that was hit, and the first part to run out wrecks the car. Your front end is armoured, your rear is not. Explosive barrels hurt everyone; chickens just splat.
    
    
    Race, jump, crash, play destruction derby and design your own tracks. Inspired by classic driving games such as Stunt Car Racer, Driver and later games like Wreckfest. The project is open source so others can explore the code, learn from it and build on it. View the source on GitHub.

      Props
      
      
      
      
      Press B (or the Barrels button), then click the map to drop a barrel. Drag one to move it, Delete removes it. Hit a barrel in the game and it starts to flash, then explodes a moment later, setting off its neighbours.
      
      
      Press K (or the Chickens button), then click the map to drop a chicken. They just splat on any touch - no explosion, no damage, purely for laughs.
      
      
      
      Bank is positive when the left edge is higher, which suits right-hand corners. Jump gaps get a takeoff ramp automatically. Lip angle curves the end of the ramp up into a kicker: 0° flattens out, higher angles throw the car higher.
