    Props
    
      
    
      
    Press B (or the Barrels button), then click the map to drop a barrel. Drag one to move it, Delete removes it. Hit a barrel in the game and it starts to flash, then explodes a moment later, setting off its neighbours.
    
      
      
    Bank is positive when the left edge is higher, which suits right-hand corners. Jump gaps get a takeoff ramp automatically. Lip angle curves the end of the ramp up into a kicker: 0° flattens out, higher angles throw the car higher.
